Northmead Primary School STEAM Programme

A practical learning programme that engaged Grade 6 learners in coding, robotics, design thinking and prototype development.

AudienceGrade 6 learners
Participation40 learners
Outputs5 functional prototypes
FocusSTEAM, coding, robotics and design thinking

Context

The programme was designed around practical exposure: giving learners a chance to engage with technology as something they could understand, build with and apply rather than only observe.

Approach

Learning activities combined coding, robotics, design thinking and guided problem solving. The emphasis was on participation, teamwork and turning ideas into working demonstrations.

Evidence

The programme engaged 40 Grade 6 learners and produced five functional prototypes. Those figures provide a clear, measurable record of participation and practical output.

Professional significance

The case demonstrates Ndanji's ability to help translate technical concepts into accessible learning experiences while maintaining a clear focus on delivery and outcomes.
