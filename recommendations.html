II
Publication standard

No anonymous praise.

Portfolio verification rule: any testimonial published here should include the recommender's name, professional role, organisation, relationship to the work and permission to publish. Where appropriate, a LinkedIn profile or institutional reference may also be linked. Until then, the portfolio relies on documented projects, roles and measurable outcomes rather than invented endorsements.
