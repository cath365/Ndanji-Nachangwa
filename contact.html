Before you reach out

Make the conversation useful.

A clear first message makes it easier to understand the opportunity and respond meaningfully.

For a project or partnership

  • What problem or opportunity are you working on?
  • Who is the programme or project intended to serve?
  • What stage are you at now?
  • What kind of support or role are you looking for?
  • What timeline are you working with?

For speaking or mentorship

  • Share the event or programme name and audience.
  • Include the proposed topic or theme.
  • State the date, location and format.
  • Explain what you would like participants to gain.
  • Include any relevant organisational details.
