Case Study • Mentorship • Girls in Technology

Technovation Girls

Volunteer coaching within a cohort of young women building confidence, collaboration and problem-solving capability through technology.

RoleVolunteer coach
Participants supported15 young women
Age range9–18
Team outcomeSenior team reached Global Semifinals

Context

Technovation Girls creates a structured environment in which girls and young women use technology and teamwork to develop solutions and build confidence in their ability to participate in innovation.

Ndanji's contribution

Ndanji served as a volunteer coach within the cohort, supporting 15 young women between the ages of 9 and 18 through mentorship and practical guidance.

Evidence

The cohort included a senior team that reached the Global Semifinals. That outcome belongs to the team and is presented here as part of the programme record, not as an individual award.

Professional significance

The case demonstrates mentorship, communication and the ability to support young participants through a structured technology programme while keeping the focus on their own learning and achievement.
