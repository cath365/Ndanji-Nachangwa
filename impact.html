Northmead Primary School • 2024–2025
STEAM Project

Ndanji led a long-form STEAM programme through Robotix Institute ZM, helping Grade 6 learners move from classroom concepts into practical coding, robotics, creativity, problem solving and design thinking.

RoleProject Lead
Audience40 Grade 6 learners
DurationJune 2024 – October 2025

Challenge

Make technology understandable, practical and exciting for young learners rather than leaving STEM as an abstract subject.

Approach

Combine coding, robotics, creativity, teamwork and design thinking with hands-on project development.

Delivery

Guide learners through concept development, problem solving, prototyping and demonstration of working ideas.

Outcome

Five functional prototypes: obstacle-avoidance robot, home automation, smart toll gate, irrigation system and smart greenhouse.

Technovation Girls • 2026 Cohort
Mentorship & coaching

Ndanji served as one of seven volunteer coaches supporting young women through an eight-week technology programme focused on confidence, teamwork and problem solving through technology.

RoleVolunteer Coach
Participants15 young women, ages 9–18
ResultSenior team reached Global Semifinals

Challenge

Create an environment where girls can participate confidently in technology, collaborate and turn ideas into solutions.

Approach

Support iterative learning, teamwork, problem framing and technology development rather than focusing only on technical output.

Leadership

Coach, encourage and help participants navigate decisions while allowing them to own their ideas and progress.

Outcome

Fifteen participants supported, with the senior team progressing to the Global Semifinals.

Rural Electrification Master Plan
55 days in the field

Working as an enumerator on the REMP upgrade connected engineering and infrastructure planning with the lived realities of communities, education access and rural development.

RoleEnumerator
Duration55 days
ContextRural electrification planning

Challenge

Support planning with information grounded in real communities and infrastructure conditions.

Approach

Field-based observation, enumeration and disciplined information capture across rural contexts.

Learning

Infrastructure decisions are strongest when technical planning is informed by accessibility, education and community realities.

Outcome

Fifty-five days of field exposure that strengthened Ndanji's understanding of engineering as a people-centred discipline.
