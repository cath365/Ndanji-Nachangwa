Programme dossier • 2024–2025

Northmead Primary School STEAM Project.

A long-form practical learning programme led through Robotix Institute ZM for Grade 6 learners, connecting coding, robotics, creativity and design thinking to tangible outputs.

Challenge

Make STEM tangible

Move technology beyond abstract classroom concepts and create a practical learning environment where learners could build, test and improve.

Role

Project Lead

Structured the learning experience, guided implementation and supported learners as they moved from ideas to working prototypes.

Outcome

Five working prototypes

An obstacle-avoidance robot, home-automation system, smart toll gate, irrigation system and smart greenhouse.

40 learners engaged from June 2024 to October 2025.
