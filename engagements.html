IV
Engagement protocol

For institutions, programmes & invitations.

A high-standard professional engagement starts with a clear brief. Organisations inviting Ndanji to participate can provide the following information to support an efficient response.

01

Purpose

State the event, programme, project or collaboration objective and why Ndanji's background is relevant.

02

Audience & stakeholders

Identify the intended audience, participating organisations and key stakeholders involved.

03

Role requested

Clarify whether the request is for speaking, mentorship, programme support, engineering input, partnership or another defined contribution.

04

Date, location & format

Include proposed timing, physical or virtual location, duration, format and any travel or logistical requirements.

05

Expected outcome

Explain what participants, the institution or the programme should gain from the engagement.

06

Official contact

Provide a responsible organisational contact and enough context for the invitation to be evaluated professionally.

Professional note: This portfolio uses a formal, diplomatic visual language to communicate standards, dignity and institutional readiness. It does not present Ndanji as a government diplomat, ambassador or holder of public office unless such a role is independently established in the future.
