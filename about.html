Purpose before position.

Ndanji Nachangwa is an Aeronautical Engineer, innovation leader and STEM education advocate whose work connects technical thinking with education, mentorship, communication and people-centred development.

Profile note

Engineering with purpose.

Her professional foundation is in aeronautical engineering, a discipline that strengthened systems thinking, analytical discipline and attention to detail. That foundation has expanded into innovation leadership, STEM education, project development, fundraising and communication.

The defining quality of the work is not a single title. It is the ability to move between technical, educational and institutional environments while keeping the purpose of the work clear.
