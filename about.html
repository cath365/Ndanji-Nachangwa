About Ndanji • Engineer • Innovator • Educator

Engineering with purpose.

Ndanji Nachangwa is an Aeronautical Engineer, innovation leader and STEM education advocate whose work sits at the intersection of engineering, technology, education and people-centred development.

Her focus is not simply on creating ideas, but on shaping practical initiatives that help people learn, participate, collaborate and access new opportunities through technology.

Based in Lusaka Province, Zambia — building at the intersection of innovation, STEM and community impact.

My Story

From engineering to innovation leadership.

My foundation is in Aeronautical Engineering, a discipline that taught me to think systematically, pay attention to detail and approach complex problems with structure. Over time, that engineering mindset expanded into innovation leadership, STEM education, communication, project development and partnership-building.

Today, my work combines technical understanding with a strong interest in people and learning. I am especially passionate about helping young people experience technology in practical ways, creating more inclusive pathways into STEM and supporting initiatives that move from concept to implementation.

Whether I am supporting a robotics activity, developing an innovation program, coordinating fundraising work or shaping communication around an initiative, I approach the work with the same goal: create clarity, build momentum and deliver something that has real value.
